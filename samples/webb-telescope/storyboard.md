# Storyboard — webb-telescope (45 s · 8 scenes · 68 beats @ 90 BPM)

Style: night-sky planetarium — deep navy-black space with a slow, twinkling parallax starfield and a faint nebula haze, a thin gold "exhibit placard" frame (label top-left, gallery counter top-right), elegant serif headlines (Noto Serif / Noto Serif KR) over a clean sans for body copy, calm `rise` entrances with `eO` deceleration, soft star-light crossfades, Webb-mirror gold as the primary accent with infrared red and cold blue as the secondaries, and the drum-less `pulse` groove at 90 BPM — because the audience is a general science-museum crowd walking through a lobby (calm, awe, readable at a distance, no mission-control jargon), the subject is literally the night sky, and a dark frame shows gold and starlight best on a large lobby screen; the default technical console HUD would read as engineering, not wonder.

Metaphor: **a museum gallery walk** — each scene is one "exhibit" of the telescope. The HUD reads like exhibit placards (`EXHIBIT 03 · THE MIRROR`); body copy stays plain language. The loop-friendly ending returns to the golden mirror of the opening, so a lobby screen that restarts the film cuts cleanly.

Audience: general public in a science-museum lobby (loop). Languages: en (default) + ko. Call to action: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/ (dock button + the golden mirror "card" on the last frame). Every number is in `facts.md`.
