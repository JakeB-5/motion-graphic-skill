# Storyboard — motion-graphic skill intro (as built, revision 3)

**Style:** flat pop — a saturated colour block per scene (yellow → blue → pink → mint → violet → orange → yellow), no frame or HUD (only a row of scene dots and a cream pill under the progress bar), huge heavy grotesk (Archivo 900, Noto Sans KR 900 for Korean; mono only for Latin labels and the install command), `slam` with the RGB split off, six different colour wipes between scenes (slant left→right, iris, top→bottom, right→left, bottom→top, iris — the old scene keeps playing outside the moving edge), electro groove at 128 BPM — because the audience is developers scrolling GitHub, the brief asked for bold, playful flat pop instead of the dark console, and `styles.md` §2 maps social teasers to exactly this.

**World:** a capsule-toy machine. You drop in a brief (coin), the material tumbles through the hopper, and a capsule pops open with one HTML file inside. The metaphor is visual only; kickers speak it (`INSERT COIN`, `HOPPER`, `PRIZE`…), body copy stays plain.

**Palette:** bg `#FFF3D6` (cream page) · ink `#16131F` · dim `#5B5670` · acc `#3D4DFF` (electric blue) · alt `#FF4F8B` (pink) · bad `#FF8A1F` (orange) · scene colours yellow `#FFD23F`, mint `#12D6A0`, violet `#7A3CFF`. Text is ink on yellow/pink/mint/orange and white on blue/violet.

**Length:** 64 beats at 128 BPM = 30.0 s (1 beat ≈ 0.469 s), 7 scenes. English + Korean (`?lang=ko`), one HTML file. CTA: `https://github.com/JakeB-5/motion-graphic-skill` (the prize ticket is the link, `CFG.endCta`).

**Changes from the approved storyboard (all logged in `review_log.md`):**
- Scene 5 is violet instead of ink (round 1: it read as the dark console); the ball that hopped on the beat was dropped when the layout was flipped (grid on top, headline at the bottom); the four-groove pills stay out (see revision 2).
- Scene 6 gained a headline, "Every film gets a scored review." (README.md:92), so the critique loop is said outright; the source line was dropped (not legible on a phone); the caption reads "0.4 beat 0.3 in blind film pairs".
- The scene-4 kicker is now `BEFORE THE BUILD · STORYBOARD` (the scene comes after the output in the film, so "STEP 03" misled); its tiles moved to the top and are larger, the headline sits in the middle.
- Scene 2 has its funnel on top and the headline at the bottom; the "Claude Code · Codex skill" chip moved above the wordmark; the install command is three lines; the "Claude Code · Codex · MIT" line is a pill at the top right of scene 7.
- A `0.4` starburst sticker sits between wordmark and machine in scene 1 (version 0.4, facts N5).
- All small pills and sublines were enlarged for phones (50–76 px on the 1920 canvas).

## Notes

- **Hook:** the wordmark slams in at 0.12 s and is readable by 0.4 s; the tagline at 0.5–1 s; the chip at 1.6 s.
- **Poster frame:** `posterT` = 2.6 s, the last beat of scene 1; the band where the play button sits (canvas y ≈ 410–680 in English, up to ≈ 790 in Korean on a phone, x < 830) is empty; the starburst is to its right.
- **Springs (F12):** shown as a light touch in scene 5 (the square and one pill) and through the springs on chips, tiles, capsule, ticket and count-up; no "closed-form", "track" or "pure function" wording on screen.
- **Honest number:** the win count is paired with its cost (13 → 43 min, "≈ 3×", a single scale); every figure is in `facts.md` with its CHANGELOG line.
