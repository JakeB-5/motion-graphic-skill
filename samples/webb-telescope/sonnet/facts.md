## Final build notes
- Kicker step numbers run STEP 02 · LIGHT … STEP 09 · EXPLORE so they match the page counter (scene 1 has no kicker). The counter changes with the kicker, about 0.37 s after each cut.
- Scene 4's ladder is a drawing, not a NASA figure: a hair's thickness cut in tenths four times (1/10, 1/100, 1/1,000, 1/10,000). Only "1/10,000 of a hair" comes from NASA (MIR).
- Scene 2's wavelength axis is logarithmic. The rainbow's colour stops follow wavelength, so 0.6 falls in the orange, inside the red end. The rainbow's own limits are not labelled with numbers.
