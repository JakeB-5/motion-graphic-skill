# Storyboard — motion-graphic skill intro (30 s, EN + KO)

**Style: flat-pop sticker bomb.** Each scene gets its own flat, saturated colour, with one faint halftone patch in a corner. Hero objects are die-cut stickers with thick white borders and hard, unblurred ink offset shadows. There's no HUD. Headlines are huge Archivo Black (Latin) or Black Han Sans (Korean) and enter word by word on springs, with a different entrance per scene; stickers land on `SPRING.playful`. Cuts are diagonal colour-block wipes, and the music is an electro groove at 128 BPM. **Why:** the brief asks for a bold, playful flat-pop look aimed at developers browsing GitHub, and a developer's laptop lid covered in stickers is where developers already advertise the tools they love.

**Metaphor (one world): the laptop-lid sticker collection.** Each scene "prints" one die-cut sticker as its hero object: the logo, the HTML file, a sound, a style card, a phone and a score. In the closing scene all six stickers slap onto a laptop lid, and the repo sticker lands last as the link. Only the kickers use the metaphor's vocabulary (`STICKER 01 · …`). Body copy stays plain.

- **Length:** 64 beats at 128 BPM (B = 0.469 s), so 30.0 s in 7 scenes (6 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 8 + 12 + 8).
- **v0.4.0's two headline features:** the scored critique loop (`score`: seven review-axis chips, nine blind pairs stamped, `9 / 9`) and springs (`style`: the line "New in v0.4: motion that settles." with a live spring-curve sticker labelled `SPRING.playful`). To make room, `style` grew from 8 to 10 beats and `outro` shrank from 10 to 8. The outro's last frame still holds after the film ends.
- **Languages:** EN (default) and KO in one file, switched with `?lang=ko`.
- **Output:** `./index.html`
- **CTA:** `https://github.com/JakeB-5/motion-graphic-skill`. The dock button label is "Open on GitHub" (EN) / "GitHub에서 보기" (KO), with the URL in `ctaSub`. The end-frame repo sticker is `CFG.endCta`.
- **Palette:** page/bg cream `#FFF3DC` · ink `#141414` · paper white `#FFFFFF` (sticker fill, text on blue) · yellow `#FFCE1F` · electric blue `#2E5BFF` · tomato `#FF4F3A` · pink `#FF7EB6`. The engine's chrome accent (`C.acc`) is ink, so the dock button, play button and progress bar read on every scene colour. A paper tape under the progress bar keeps it visible everywhere.
- **Scene backgrounds:** yellow → blue → tomato → pink → yellow → blue → cream (for the lid). Each scene uses one accent.
- **Type:** display is Archivo Black + Black Han Sans (KO). Text is Noto Sans KR 700/900. JetBrains Mono is used only for Latin code (`index.html`, the `npx` command, sound names, `SPRING.playful`).
- **Music:** `electro`, C–G–Am–F. `arpScenes: ['beat', 'style']`. No lite scenes, so the `9 / 9` payoff sits on the full groove. `impact` plays only twice: the file becoming a film in `brief`, and the repo sticker in `outro`.

## Changes during build (from the approved storyboard)

Changes to layout and motion only. The story, scenes, messages, numbers and link are as approved.

- **Output path** is `./index.html`, as the build instructions asked. The file sticker in `brief` reads `index.html` to match (it read `film.html` in the approved version).
- **Scene colours:** `style` is pink, not ink/black. The round-1 critique found that a near-black scene read as the "default dark console" the brief ruled out. `phone` therefore moved from pink to yellow. The palette itself is unchanged.
- **Headline entrances:** the slams became word-by-word spring entrances, one kind per scene (round-1 critique, D6: headlines popped in or only faded).
- **Layouts:** `style` and `score` flipped to object-left, copy-right (round-1 critique: one layout repeated across scenes).
- **Cuts:** three of the six diagonal wipes became cuts grown from the scene: an iris out of the film's play button, blinds, and the phone screen growing to full frame (round-2 critique: the same wipe at every cut). Every scene's first entrance now starts at 0 s, inside the cut (round-2 critique, D5).
- **`hello`:** added the promise line "Motion graphics / from a brief." top-left (round-2 critique: the first 2 s only showed the name), a star badge at beat 3, a stronger beat bounce, and kicks and hats building into the groove. The sticker is 860 px wide, centred at x 1360.
- **`phone`:** the approved `7/7 ✓` badge became seven device outlines, each with a check, popping on beats 3.5–5 after the sub at beat 3, so the full row holds before the cut (round-3 critique). Each headline line travels in as one piece (round-3 critique, D1: words collided).
- **`score`:** added the seven review axes (README:92) as two rows of 36 px chips led by a "7 axes" label, so the critique feature reads as more than a kicker and survives a phone screen (round-3 critique, D2). The detail line is split over two lines, and it is no longer a lite scene (its level was half the others). The `9 / 9` hit is a single `slam`.
- **`outro`:** the full URL is on the repo sticker at display size, and the command is 44 px mono on two lines. Timings moved earlier (minis 0.2–2.2, repo sticker at beat 3, typing 3.5–5) so the end frame holds ~1.4 s. The headline runs as one line across the top, with the lid centred under it and the command left-aligned under the lid (round-1 critique D2; round-2 critique: repeated layouts).
- **`style`:** the spring-curve sticker and the springs line arrive at beat 3 with the CLEAN morph (approved: beat 4.5), to fill the right column sooner; words grow from their centre (round-3 critique, D1).
- **KO springs line:** "v0.4 새 기능: / 자연스럽게 멈추는 움직임." on two lines (approved: "v0.4: 자연스럽게 멈추는 움직임.").
