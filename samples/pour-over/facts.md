## Where sources disagree, and which one is used

- **Water temperature.** Hario: 92–96 °C. SCA-310: 90–96 °C. **Used: Hario's 92–96 °C** as the instruction (it is the
  V60-specific guidance and sits entirely inside the SCA range). The SCA 90–96 °C range appears only on the detail line
  as context ("inside the SCA's 90–96 °C range"). Same strings in every scene and language.
- **Coffee-to-water ratio.** Hario's recipe (15 g : 250 g) is slightly stronger than the SCA's 55 g per kg test ratio.
  **Used: Hario's 15 g and 250 g** as the recipe. The SCA 55 g per litre figure is shown as a labelled reference on the
  detail line (honest companion), never mixed into the recipe numbers. (55 g/kg of water ≈ 55 g per litre; the SCA itself
  allows "its equivalent in other units".) No derived ratio (e.g. 1:17) is shown.
- **Water volume vs. mass.** Hario lists "250ml water" and "Stop at 250g total weight". **Used: "250 g"** (it is what the
  scale reads).
- **Brew time.** Hario: approximately 2.5 minutes. SCA-310 gives no contact time for manual pour-over (secondary sites
  quote 4–8 min from the older Golden Cup brewer standard — not used). **Used: Hario's "about 2½ min"** / "약 2분 30초".
- **Bloom water amount.** Hario gives no gram figure ("enough to thoroughly wet grounds"), so no number is shown for it.
